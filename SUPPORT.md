# Support

The Clean Compute Engine is pre-calibration research software, not a hosted
service or validated engineering tool.

- Use **GitHub Discussions** for modeling questions, architecture ideas, and
  contributor coordination.
- Use **GitHub Issues** for reproducible defects and scoped research proposals.
- Use the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) for vulnerabilities,
  credentials, restricted data, or sensitive facility information.

Do not submit confidential information or operational telemetry through any
support channel. Maintainers cannot provide permitting, financial, emissions,
water, reliability, or facility-design advice based on the current model.
